Who we are

Frequently asked

Questions, answered clearly.

Starting points for understanding our services and how we work.

What services does S.M. Securities provide?

Share trading and demat accounts, commodity trading, derivatives trading, portfolio management services, financial planning, mutual funds, life insurance, general insurance and loans.

Do you provide personalised financial solutions?

Yes. Our approach centres on solutions aligned with individual goals, requirements, risk appetite and long-term aspirations.

Does S.M. Securities provide investment education?

Yes. Financial education is an important part of our mission — seminars, webinars, training and investor-awareness initiatives.

Do you guarantee investment returns?

No. Investment returns cannot be guaranteed. Financial markets and investment products carry risk, which investors should understand before deciding.

How do I start my investment journey?

Begin by understanding your financial goals and requirements. Contact our team to discuss them and explore suitable options.

Can beginners approach S.M. Securities?

Yes. Investors at any stage can ask for information and guidance about the services and solutions available.

Why is financial planning important?

It helps you organise goals, understand requirements, evaluate risk and build a structured approach to your financial future.
